Help at home

Arrange a ride

Start with the trip you need to make. Your local aging agency can help you look for transportation in your area.

Where to start

Contact the Eldercare Locator with your town or ZIP code. Ask for the local agency that helps older adults arrange rides.

How to start the conversation

“I need a ride from ____ to ____ on ____. Who serves that route, and how far ahead should I book?”

Questions to take with you

  • Can I book a return trip at the same time?
  • How much will the round trip cost?
  • Can I bring a walker, wheelchair, or someone to help me?
